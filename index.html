Where are you in the purchase?

The best moment to call us is before you sign anything.

Until you sign a binding offer, pay a reservation deposit or sign a deposit contract (contrato de arras, the private contract that fixes price and deadlines before the deed), you can still ask questions, set conditions and walk away. Afterwards your options are narrower.

Nothing signed yetThis is when analysis is most useful: what we find can still shape your offer and your conditions.
Reservation paidWe first look at what you signed and which deadlines are running, then tell you honestly what can be delivered in the time left.
Deposit contract signedWe can still help you understand the property and what you have committed to. We cannot undo a commitment; whether terms can still change is assessed by a lawyer within ADVISE.

Questions

What buyers ask first.

Do you search for properties?

No. We start when you have found a property. We do not sell, list or search.

Can you guarantee the property has no problems?

No, and nobody can. We report what the documents and the inspection show, say where information is missing, and leave open what cannot be checked.

I have already paid a reservation. Is it too late?

Not necessarily. We can still analyse the property and explain what you have committed to. We will tell you before you engage us if your deadline is too short for the work to be useful.

Is CHECK enough if I am about to sign?

Usually not. CHECK identifies risks from documents; it does not analyse them and includes no inspection. If you are close to signing, we will normally recommend INSPECT or ADVISE.

Where do you work?
